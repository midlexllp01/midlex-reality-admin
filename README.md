## 📸 Property Media Upload Guide (Images & Videos)

When uploading listings to Midlex Realty via link, use the recommended free hosting platforms below to get links that work reliably across the platform:

### 1. Where to Upload Images (Photos)
To display properly on the marketplace, your link needs to be a **Direct Image Link** (ending in `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, or `.webp`).

#### Option A: Postimages (Recommended – Fastest & Easiest)
* **Website**: [postimages.org](https://postimages.org)
* **Cost**: 100% Free (No signup required)
* **How to use**:
  1. Go to [postimages.org](https://postimages.org).
  2. Click **Choose images** and select your property photos.
  3. Once uploaded, copy the link from the box labeled **"Direct link"** (example: `https://i.postimg.cc/xyz123/property.jpg`).
  4. Paste that link into your Midlex Admin panel.

#### Option B: ImgBB
* **Website**: [imgbb.com](https://imgbb.com)
* **Cost**: Free (No signup required)
* **How to use**:
  1. Click **Start Uploading** and upload your images.
  2. Select **Don't autodelete**.
  3. Under *Embed codes*, choose **Direct links** and copy the URL (example: `https://i.ibb.co/abc/living-room.jpg`).

#### Option C: Cloudinary (Best for Professional Real Estate)
* **Website**: [cloudinary.com](https://cloudinary.com)
* **Cost**: Free tier includes 25GB storage and bandwidth.
* **Benefit**: High speed, automatic quality optimization, and hosts both images and videos.

---

### 2. Where to Upload Videos (Virtual Tours & Walkthroughs)
Your website's video player is already built to play **YouTube**, **Vimeo**, and **Direct MP4** videos.

#### Option A: YouTube (Recommended – Free & Unlimited)
* **Website**: [youtube.com](https://youtube.com)
* **Cost**: 100% Free with unlimited uploads and 4K quality.
* **How to use**:
  1. Upload your walkthrough video to your YouTube account.
  2. In the visibility settings, choose **Unlisted** *(this ensures it will NOT appear publicly on YouTube search; only visitors on Midlex Realty can watch it)*.
  3. Copy the video link (e.g., `https://youtu.be/abc123xyz` or `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=abc123xyz`).
  4. Paste it into the **Video Tour URL** box in Midlex Admin.

#### Option B: Vimeo
* **Website**: [vimeo.com](https://vimeo.com)
* **Cost**: Free tier available.
* Clean, ad-free video player. Just copy the video URL (e.g., `https://vimeo.com/123456789`) and paste it.

#### Option C: Streamable
* **Website**: [streamable.com](https://streamable.com)
* Quick drag-and-drop video uploads with instant embed links.

---

* **For Videos**: Upload to [youtube.com](https://youtube.com) as **Unlisted** → Copy the video link.
